1. YOUR QUESTION: "Why don't you get any negative classes for A5, A6 and A7?"

It is your branch 2 — a scoping bug in window-truth computation, not
saturation. Your framing anticipated it exactly; the defect is entirely on our
side of the pipeline, and it needed no HPC data to find.

The evaluator scores against test_y.npy (the spike-based label), not the
leak-free y_indep. For the HF variants that label includes

    ddiv_spike = attack_v in HF_VARIANTS & (d_div > 1)

and d_div > 1 holds in 92-96% of ALL rows, dormant cycles included. Window
truth is max() over a 10-cycle window, so nearly every window is labelled
positive, TN and FP both collapse to 0, and the MCC denominator becomes zero.

    variant   rows d_div>1   windows positive from that leg alone   TN
    A5           91.7 %                 100.0 %                      0
    A6           96.4 %                 100.0 %                      0
    A7           91.9 %                 100.0 %                      0
    A8           54.1 %                  92.6 %                    173

A8 is the control: its lower rate leaves 7.4% of windows able to stay
negative, which is exactly why it alone has a nonzero TN.

Relabelled against y_indep — which the pipeline already computes and saves —
the same test split carries 57-72% negative windows. I have NOT applied that
change, as it moves every A5-A8 number and you reserved the decision. No MCC
for A5/A6/A7 appears anywhere in the report.

Two corrections to what I told you previously: the A5-A7 data was on the local
machine all along (the "zero rows" was the preprocessed cache, not the source),
and the local reruns were never contradicting the pooled result.

3. ITEM 9 — THE RESIDUAL IS SYSTEMIC, NOT THREE NODES

The gate repoint is in and correct. But you asked me to confirm no similar
residuals appear elsewhere, and they do, extensively.

Per cycle on A5 @60%, the gate (hf_send_gt > 0) is nonzero in ~10% of cycles
while S5 fires in ~45-53%. They do not match: hf_send_gt increments only when
an RSU schedules a hidden duplicate, and S5's firing condition is true far
more often than that.

Sweeping every node with S5 activity: 39 nodes show the residual, and 36 of
them are NOT 220/225/233. Several are worse than the three I reported to you
(node 236: 166 residual cycles; node 227: 157; node 219: 154 — against node
220's 119). Those three were simply the ones I had looked at; I should have
swept the full set before reporting them as three isolated cases.

This is the same class of defect as the finding above — window truth derived
from a signal whose granularity does not match the detector — but in the
opposite direction: that one labels almost everything positive, this labels
too much negative. I have not changed the gate further, as the fix depends on
what you consider the correct send-side truth signal.
