### P4 — Handoff-fix mechanical writeup (paper framing)

The question is whether the fix is a new component or a corrected
implementation of `eq:mobility_baseline`.

**Input from the diagnosis, for whoever writes it up:** `s1_detect_packet()`
adds a 50–300 ms handoff-jitter draw to a packet's delay and then tests that
inflated value against its own threshold — so S1 was firing on a confounder the
detector injects itself (94.8% of zero-attack false positives fall inside that
band, median firing delay 246.9 ms). The confounder is *larger* than the 80 ms
A1/A2 inject, which is why no threshold could separate them and why the
best-effort arm at ~151 ms rejected true detections along with jitter.

That reads as **a corrected implementation**, not a new component: the paper
already models handoff jitter as legitimate latency; the detector simply was
not excluding it from its own evidence. Framing it as a new component would
imply the equation was incomplete, when the equation was fine and the
implementation double-counted.
