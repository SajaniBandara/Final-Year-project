Which variants fall short, and why (Q6, composite column). Only A6 clears 0.80
(0.808).

- A1 (0.513) and A2 (0.615) are limited by false positives.
  - Recall is 0.97 and 0.98, but precision only 0.43 and 0.64.
  - On A1 the primary detector (S2f) causes most of them itself: 313 false
    positives in score_primary, a 24 % false-positive rate.
  - On A2 the primary detector is at 0.917. The composite adds about 300 false
    positives from other detectors firing during the run.

- A3 (0.549) and A4 (0.589) are limited by missed detections in the TCAM detector
  (S3/S4).
  - Primary recall is 0.58 and 0.75. A4's primary detector has no false positives
    at all (precision 1.000).
  - The composite adds 88 and 170 false positives on top.

- A5 (0.700), A7 (0.704) and A8 (0.785) are limited by false positives in the
  composite: recall at least 0.96, precision 0.67-0.80.
  - On A5 the primary detector (S5) finds only 48 % of attack blocks. The
    composite recovers recall through other detectors at a cost in precision.
  - On A7 the primary detector (S7) itself has 296 false positives (precision
    0.62).
  - A8 is 0.015 short.

What the LSTM adds (Q6 vs Q5, composite):
- It adds true positives on every variant (+1 to +23) and false positives on every
  variant (+6 to +21).
- The net effect is a gain on A1 (+0.012) and A2 (+0.031), and a loss on A3-A8
  (-0.005 to -0.020).
- That is why Q6 lands 0.0035 below Q5.
- On score_primary the LSTM, witness and BTMM layers change nothing: Q5 and Q6
  are identical.

5. variant_active()
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Done. It is a set-backed (bitmask) predicate that replaces every
active_attack_variant == N test in all four detectors. The mask lives in routing.cc
beside active_attack_variant, because attack_declaration.h is included before
crypto_layer.h and populates it. An empty mask falls back to the old single-value
test, so benign and baseline runs are unchanged. NEXUS N1-N3 are no longer blocked
by the single-variant assumption.


6. W
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your call taken: the code keeps S6_WINDOW_S = 30.0, and the paper now says 30 s. The
old justification cited a {5,10,15} s candidate range and a W <= 9 s bound, so it
was rewritten too. It now explains why the detection window has to be wider than the
10 s evaluation block: at 10 s, duplication would go unseen whenever the two
receptions fall in different blocks. It also states, as a limitation, that a window
this long often spans an RSU handoff.


7. OPTION (b): STOP ASSERTING ON QUARANTINED NODES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implemented, and on in every run in the table above.

It was corrected once during validation. The first version also checked the
accused vehicle's covering RSU. That is the right test for enforcement but the
wrong one for suppressing detections. With 44 RSUs quarantined in A6, one RSU's
quarantine silenced every accusation against every vehicle in its zone: A6 fell to
TP = 0 and MCC 0.0000, with all 33 true positives turned into false negatives.
Checking the accused node alone restores it (0.0000 -> 0.1219).

Measured cost, logged as a limitation (HF variants, macro, 90 s diagnostic):

  option (b) off                 0.2808
  option (b) on (corrected)      0.2632
  option (b) on + latch reset    0.2599

So about -0.018 macro, almost exactly the latch reset's -0.0168 at 300 s. The
approval assumed the system has stopped treating a quarantined node as compromised.
That only holds if the truth latch is also released at quarantine. Otherwise truth
keeps counting the node as positive, and silencing the detector turns true
positives into false negatives.


8. A6's 248-NODE QUARANTINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Explained, and it is not a separate defect: S6 had the same overhearing problem as
S5. Once the addressing conjunct landed, A6 quarantine fell from 248 to 88 and S6
firings from 96,912 to 121 (90 s). With enforcement off, the same run gives 2,810
S6 firings. That gap is option (b) suppressing accusations against nodes that are
already contained.

10. DISCLOSURES AND LIMITATIONS  (logged, not chased)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) The LSTM deployed before this round was the end-to-end classifier head with
   the encoder unfrozen, the one recorded on 1 Sep as "present it, don't ship it".
   - Its weights match global_clshead_indep_e2e.pt exactly, and cls_theta.json was
     that checkpoint's offline threshold block.
   - An in-sim recalibration had been produced but was never put in use.
   - LSTM_HC_THETA had been calibrated on a different encoder.
   - No reported number used this model. Every Q1-Q6 and ablation-arm result
     predates its export (1 Sep 10:19). Nothing since has enabled LSTM inference,
     and the round 9 and 10 tables used score_primary, which excludes the LSTM.
   - The retrain replaces it with the frozen head. The classifier thresholds and
     both HC thresholds now come from the model that ships.

b) Option (b) costs about -0.018 macro at 90 s (section 7).

c) Enforcement is off in every Q config, so the table measures detection only.
   - enable_quarantine_enforcement defaults to false and no Q config sets it. A
     quarantine is recorded but never blocks forwarding.
   - As a result, UCR is identical in all seven configs: A5 84.63 %, A6 91.35 %,
     A7 84.89 %, A8 93.65 %.
   - For A3 and A5-A8, PDR and UCR are identical at every one of the 298 cycles
     between Q1 (no HF detection) and the regeneration run (full system).
   - The enforcement effect was measured separately in round 10, with enforcement
     on at 90 s.
   - UCR by intensity was not run this round and is kept for final experiments.

d) The M8 poisoning sweep was not re-run (it was not in this round's list), so M8
   is stale against the new features.

e) Paper label audit, beyond the four labels you asked us to confirm:
   - sec:fed_lstm was a second live duplicate (now fixed). Our round 10 note
     wrongly said eq:theta_adapt was the only one.
   - eq:theta_adapt still has two live, different definitions. We recommend
     keeping line 3472 (the k-sigma form), because s1_use_percentile is off by
     default, which makes k-sigma the version the code actually runs.

f) Result files overwritten by appends.
   - Training runs write untagged filenames, and the logger appends to a file that
     already exists. 223 of 504 MOBIGUARD_*.csv files contain rows from more than
     one build.
   - The Q1-Q6 runner also writes untagged names and renames each file after its
     run. So the seed-5 Q1 files at 60 % also swallowed the regeneration's seed-5
     rows. We preserved copies first, and the collector reads only the last
     complete run in each file.
   - The Q1-Q6 table reads detector_windows files, which are overwritten on every
     write, so it is unaffected.
   - Training CSVs were checked before the retrain resumed and are clean: all
     14,400 files hold exactly 298 rows of a single schema.
   - The offline evaluator first crashed on the mixed rows. It was then run on a
     clean copy holding one complete regeneration run per file.
   - A first rerun also picked up in-flight Q1 rows, which gave A3 0.941 and A4
     0.842. The corrected figures in section 9 supersede them.
   - evaluator.py's M4-M8 section pools every MOBIGUARD file for a variant and
     intensity, so its offline M4-M8 figures should not be quoted.

g) Per-RSU joint calibration misses its precision target at every RSU. During
   federated training, fed_aggregator.py tries to find each RSU's threshold that
   reaches precision >= 0.95 inside the FPR budget. After this retrain none of
   the 64 RSUs reaches it (mean precision 0.730), and each falls back to the
   lowest-FPR option. The warning is not new, and it has been in the code since
   7 Jul: it fired 58 times on 9 Jul and 43 times on 9 Aug, so this run is worse.

h) The ablation configs cannot isolate every component.
   - Q4 ("witness only") scores exactly 0 by construction. D_RSU is S2f, S5, S6,
     S7, S8 and the LSTM, and Q4 switches all of them off. The witness mechanism
     acts through signed alerts and BFT trust penalties, which the window score
     does not read.
   - Q2 scores 0 on A1-A4 because it disables S1-S4.
   - Q3 is near 0 on A3/A4 (recall 0.02 and 0.01). The LSTM is suppressed at any
     RSU where a rule already covers the event: the raw S3/S4 condition, or the
     raw R_anom rule. That gate deliberately ignores the disable flags. So "LSTM
     only" never acts on TCAM-covered RSUs, and on HF variants it is also silent
     wherever R_anom fires.
   - Q4R scores only through R_anom, and on score_primary only on A7/A8.

i) Single seed. Differences of a few thousandths between configs, including Q6 vs
   Q5, are not established by this table.

j) Classifier-path thresholds are calibrated in-sample. Both cls_theta.json and
   LSTM_HC_THETA_CLS were fitted, and their firing rates measured, on the same three
   benign runs. No held-out benign run was scored.

k) The offline evaluator scores the autoencoder's reconstruction path, not the
   classifier head that runs in the simulator.
   - Its A5-A8 rows use hf_theta.json from 20 Aug, fitted on the previous model
     with zero A5 and zero A6 calibration windows, and not recalibrated this round.
   - The offline figure for the shipped path is the frozen head's 0.4678
     (section 9).

l) The Q1-Q6 runner does not add the config tag to lambda_l_true diagnostics for A1,
   A3 and A4, so each config overwrote the previous one's. No reported number reads
